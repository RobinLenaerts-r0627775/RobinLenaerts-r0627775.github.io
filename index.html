I was responsible for designing the architecture, leading the development team, and ensuring the successful integration of multiple systems.

IoT project

During the last project week at UCLL we (a group of 4 second year students) made a people counter with a Raspberry Pi and a webcam in a week's time. We developed this project based on an open source Python script. It got us a long way but was far from perfect so we had to make a lot of adjustments for the script to suit our needs.

Technology in Healthcare Education: DementiApp

https://github.com/RobinLenaerts-r0627775/DementiApp
For my graduation project I developed an application with two other students. It was a mobile application aimed to improve the life quality for dementia patients by increasing social interaction with the help of reminiscense. We used Xamarin as well as Spring Boot to achieve this.

VR experience for AB InBev onboarding (internship)


As an intern I worked at AB-INBEV for three months. I developed a VR application that is used for on-boarding and recruiting to give potential recruits a feel for the atmosphere within the company. This included virtual brewery tours for various breweries and warehouses, as well as a a little game where you can mix ingredients to brew your own beer. The application is made in Unity.
